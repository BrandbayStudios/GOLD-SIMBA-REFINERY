What We Do

Our Services

Integrated mineral refining, processing, assaying, and laboratory testing services designed to deliver accuracy, transparency, and high-quality results across the mineral value chain.

LOT 01

Gold Refining

Professional gold refining services aimed at recovering and producing high-purity gold from gold-bearing materials, maximizing recovery while maintaining strict quality and security standards.

LOT 02

Mineral Assaying & Testing

Reliable analytical testing and assaying for gold and other mineral samples, determining composition, grade, purity, and other characteristics to support accurate valuation.

LOT 03

Gold Purity & Fineness Testing

Detailed testing to establish the purity and fineness of gold, providing dependable results for trading, valuation, refining, and certification purposes.

LOT 04

Precious & Base Metal Analysis

Laboratory analysis extending beyond gold to precious and base metals, helping clients understand the composition and commercial value of their mineral products.

LOT 05

Mineral Sample Preparation

Professional preparation, handling, and processing of mineral samples prior to laboratory analysis, ensuring representative and dependable results.

LOT 06

Independent Laboratory Verification

Independent testing and verification for clients who require an objective assessment of mineral quality, grade, and composition before entering commercial transactions.

LOT 07

Refinery & Laboratory Consultation

Technical guidance on mineral refining, laboratory testing, assay interpretation, quality control, and processing requirements to support informed decisions.

LOT 08

Mineral Valuation Support

Accurate laboratory analysis and purity determination assisting clients with reliable technical information for valuation, purchase, sale, financing, and export.

LOT 09

Quality Control & Compliance Testing

Systematic mineral testing and analytical verification supporting businesses in maintaining consistent quality and meeting commercial and regulatory requirements.

LOT 10

Buying on Behalf of Client

Acting as a trusted purchasing agent, sourcing and buying gold and other minerals on behalf of clients under agreed terms, verified pricing, and full transactional transparency.

LOT 11

Gold Biometric Security System

Biometric access control and identity verification safeguarding mineral custody, vault access, and every stage of the handling process.

LOT 12

Client Consultation

One-on-one consultation guiding clients — from first-time sellers to institutional traders — through the mineral testing, refining, and trading process.

GOLDSIMBA REFINERY

A professionally driven mineral refinery and testing laboratory delivering trusted refining, assaying, and value-addition services across Zambia and the wider African minerals market.
