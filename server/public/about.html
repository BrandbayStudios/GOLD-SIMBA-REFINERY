About the Company

Precision. Purity. Trust.

Gold SIMBA Refinery is a professionally driven mineral refining and testing laboratory dedicated to reliable, secure, and high-quality services across the minerals and precious metals value chain.

Our core business focuses on the refining, processing, assaying, and laboratory testing of minerals and precious metals, with a strong emphasis on accuracy, transparency, integrity, and compliance with applicable industry standards. We work with miners, mineral traders, investors, exporters, and other stakeholders who require dependable mineral evaluation and refining solutions.

Through modern testing methodologies, controlled procedures, and qualified personnel, we strive to maintain a high standard of analytical reliability — while our refining operations focus on recovering and upgrading valuable metals to commercially acceptable standards under strict controls throughout.

We understand that trust is the foundation of every successful mineral transaction, and place great importance on confidentiality, chain-of-custody procedures, responsible handling, and transparent reporting. Our ambition is to establish Gold SIMBA Refinery as a trusted regional centre for mineral refining, assaying, and laboratory testing — serving Zambia and the wider African minerals market.

Vision & Mission

What drives the refinery

Vision

To become a leading and internationally trusted mineral refinery and testing laboratory, renowned for purity, precision, integrity, and innovation — setting industry standards in mineral processing, refining, and laboratory analysis while contributing to the responsible growth of the African minerals sector and beyond.

Mission

To provide world-class mineral refining, assaying, and laboratory testing services that deliver accurate, reliable, and transparent results — applying modern technology, rigorous quality control, and professional expertise to build lasting confidence among miners, traders, investors, governments, and international partners.
