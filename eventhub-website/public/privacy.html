Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: we collect the details you give us so you can create an account, contact vendors and plan events. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking or analytics tools.

This policy explains how EventHub Zambia ("EventHub", "we", "us") handles personal information when you use our website at events.tiltsolutions.tech and the EventHub Zambia mobile app (together, the "Service"). The Service connects people planning events in Zambia with event vendors such as venues, decorators, photographers and caterers.

We handle personal information in line with the Data Protection Act, No. 3 of 2021, of Zambia.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password when you register. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form and we cannot read them.
  • Quote requests and event plans: your name, phone number, event type, date, location, guest count and any requirements you write.
  • Vendor applications: business name, contact person, phone, email, category, location, business description and, if you choose, a Facebook page link.
  • Reviews you write about vendors.

Information collected automatically:

  • Session cookies that keep you signed in and protect forms against misuse. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which our server records when you visit and stores with your login session.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and keep it secure.
  • To send your quote requests to the vendor you choose, and to show vendors the requests made to them.
  • To review vendor applications and publish approved vendor profiles.
  • To help you plan your event and to follow up on event plans you submit.
  • To respond to your messages and provide support.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to meet our legal obligations.

3. Who can see your information

  • Vendors: when you request a quote, the vendor receives the details in your request, including your name and phone number, so they can reply to you.
  • Public vendor profiles: if you are a vendor, your business name, category, location, description, photos, packages, phone number for WhatsApp contact and reviews are shown publicly once your profile is approved.
  • EventHub administrators can see accounts, vendor applications, quote requests and event plans in order to run the Service.
  • Service providers: our website and data are hosted by Hostinger. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Other services you reach through EventHub

Some features take you to other companies' services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • WhatsApp opens when you tap a WhatsApp button to contact a vendor.
  • Facebook, Google Search and Google Maps open in a new tab when you use "Search Facebook", "Search Google" or "Google Maps". EventHub only passes along the words and city you searched for.
  • Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, icons from jsDelivr, photos from Unsplash and a map from Google Maps, so those services receive your IP address when you view a page.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Quote requests and event plans are kept for up to two years so you and vendors can refer back to them, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

6. How we protect your information

The Service uses encrypted HTTPS connections, passwords are hashed, and only authorised administrators can see account and request data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia.

8. Children

The Service is meant for adults planning events and running event businesses. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will place a notice on the Service.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact EventHub Zambia through the EventHub website or by email at privacy@tiltsolutions.tech.
