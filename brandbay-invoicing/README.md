# BrandBay Invoicing

A standalone invoicing system for BrandBay Innovation Studios, built in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It has no server or database, and no install step.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html`, or upload the folder (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`) to any web host, such as a folder on your Hostinger site like `brandbaystudios.online/invoicing`.

## Features

- **Documents:** invoices, quotations, proforma invoices and receipts, with automatic numbering (`INV-2026-0001`, `QUO-…`, `PRO-…`, `REC-…`). You can convert a quotation or proforma into an invoice, duplicate a document, and issue a receipt from a paid invoice.
- **All BrandBay services and sub-services:** the six service categories with their sub-services (Logo Design, SEO, EPOS Setup, PACRA registration, Business Cards…). Set your standard prices in *Services & prices* and add your own categories. Custom lines are always allowed.
- **Tax types:** VAT 16%, zero-rated, exempt, Turnover Tax, No tax, and Withholding Tax, which is deducted from the amount payable. Choose the tax per line; tax is calculated after discounts and summarised by type. You can edit the rates and add new ones. Confirm current rates with ZRA.
- **Currencies:** ZMW, USD, ZAR, GBP and EUR, and you can add more. Give a currency an exchange rate and its invoices also show the Kwacha equivalent.
- **Banking details:** multiple bank accounts (bank, account name and number, branch, sort code, SWIFT) and Mobile Money (MTN, Airtel, Zamtel). Pick which ones appear on each document.
- **Payments:** record partial or full payments. The status changes to Partially paid, Paid or Overdue automatically, and an invoice can show the 70% deposit / 30% balance split.
- **Clients:** saved client list with TPIN, address and outstanding balances.
- **Output:** A4 print and Save as PDF, an email draft, and CSV export of all documents.
- **Dashboard:** totals for invoiced, received, outstanding and overdue amounts.

## Before your first invoice

1. **Settings:** add your bank account and Mobile Money numbers, your TPIN and, optionally, your logo.
2. **Services & prices:** set your standard prices (they start at 0).

## Your data

Everything is saved in the browser you use, on that computer only. Clearing your browser data erases it. Use **Settings → Export backup** regularly, and use **Import backup** to restore your data or move it to another computer or browser.
