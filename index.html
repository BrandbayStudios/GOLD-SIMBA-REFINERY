Who we are

A Zambian supply partner you can count on

Mijeko Resources Zambia Limited is a citizen-owned private company based in Makeni, Lusaka. We source and deliver lubricants, cooking oil, animal feed, soya chunks and other general supplies to businesses, institutions and public bodies across Zambia.

Our products are delivered according to each customer's requirements and specifications, at competitive prices, and we are always open to negotiating the best outcome for every order.

Our mission

Management and employees are committed to service excellence, delivering quality products and service with continuous improvement, while maximising value for our customers and shareholders.

Our vision

To be a trusted supplier of everyday essentials to Zambian businesses and institutions, known for reliable delivery, fair pricing and strong customer relationships.

How we work

Safety, environment & quality

Quality Management System Policy

Our goal is to implement quality management systems and processes that deliver the highest practicable quality of products and services to meet client expectations. We aim to deliver a consistently high level of service across sales, marketing and the sourcing of products locally and around the globe. Our quality management system guides continuous improvement and increases customer satisfaction.

Health, Safety & Environmental Policy

We are committed to industry best practice in environmental performance, through sustainable management practices and continuous improvement. We will:

  1. Comply with applicable environmental, health and safety laws, regulations and standards.
  2. Identify, assess and manage environmental, health and safety risks in our operations.
  3. Set objectives and targets for continuous improvement.
  4. Require every employee to take personal responsibility for meeting our objectives.
  5. Educate and train employees to include environmental considerations in their work.
  6. Design our processes with the health and safety of employees, clients and the public in mind.
  7. Communicate regularly with the communities where we operate.
  8. Promote the conservation of energy and natural resources and reduce waste.
  9. Routinely monitor and report on our environmental, health and safety performance.
