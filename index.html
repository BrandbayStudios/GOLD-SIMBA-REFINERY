Who we are

A Zambian partner for the mining industry

Mijeko Resources Zambia Limited is a Zambian-owned private company based in Lusaka. We provide mining services and supplies, mechanical repairs and maintenance, electrical and instrumentation spares and installations, and civil and mechanical engineering works for the mining industry.

We are committed to providing professional solutions to our clients, together with up-to-date technical solutions that meet each customer's specific needs. Our competent and experienced team, working with engineers across the industry, makes sure we deliver to client expectations.

Our mission

Management and employees are committed to service excellence, delivering a quality service and continuous improvement to processes in mining and allied industries, while maximising shareholder value.

Our vision

To be a control and instrumentation solution provider to all our clients, and an industry leader in customer relations, sales, service, plant automation and repair of process control equipment.

How we work

Safety, environment & quality

Health, Safety & Environmental Policy

We are committed to industry best practice in environmental performance, through environmentally sustainable management practices and continuous improvement. We aim to eliminate injuries, occupational illnesses, unsafe practices and environmental harm from our operations. We will:

  1. Comply with applicable environmental, health and safety laws, regulations and international standards.
  2. Identify, assess and manage environmental, health and safety risks of planned and existing operations.
  3. Set objectives and targets for continuous improvement.
  4. Require every employee to take personal responsibility for meeting our objectives.
  5. Provide the leadership and resources our workforce needs to meet our targets.
  6. Educate and train employees to include environmental considerations in all their work.
  7. Design all processes and systems of work with the health and safety of employees, clients and the public in mind.
  8. Communicate regularly with the communities where we operate.
  9. Promote the conservation of energy and natural resources and reduce waste.
  10. Routinely monitor, assess and report on our environmental, health and safety performance.
Quality Management System Policy

Our goal is to implement quality management systems and processes that deliver the highest practicable quality of products and services to meet client expectations. We aim to deliver a consistently high level of service across sales, marketing, installation services and the sourcing of products from around the globe. Our quality management system guides continuous improvement and increases customer satisfaction.

Opportunities & partnerships

We seek unique partnerships with leading companies worldwide, which we call our Health Growth Partnership. This helps us meet increasing international competition and the demand for services and equipment support, so that we can offer the best solutions in the mining industry.
